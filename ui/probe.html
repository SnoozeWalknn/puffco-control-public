Peak Pro — device probe

Answers whether this unit can run temperature curves, before any app is built.

Before you start

  1. Close the Puffco app on your phone and turn its Bluetooth off. A Peak accepts one connection at a time — if the phone holds it, nothing else can see the device.
  2. Load a chamber. Nothing else needs to be on it.
  3. Hold the power button ~5 s until the logo glows blue (pairing mode). Pairing mode times out after a couple of minutes.
  4. Tap Connect. The picker now lists every BLE device nearby, so look for your Peak by name — it may appear as a 12-character string of hex rather than a friendly name.
Tests 1–4 are read-only or write harmless values and restore them. Only the final test heats, and it asks first.

Run a real curve
Waits for the chamber to settle near the profile temperature, then drives the setpoint offset for 20 s while sampling. This is the only part that heats, and it clears the offset and aborts on its own when it finishes.
